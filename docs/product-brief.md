# Product brief

## Agreed direction

- Name: Paasaa.ai.
- Motto: your best space.
- Intended audience: people aged 13 and up experiencing anxiety. This broad audience is a product intention, not evidence that one intervention treats every anxiety disorder.
- Work incrementally. Begin with the breathing opening screen; address CBT separately.
- Appearance: calm white and blue; a natural, human-feeling upper-body illustration rather than a robotic or uncanny avatar.
- Greeting and clock use the visitor's device-local time. No location access is needed.

## Opening screen

1. Brand and motto, local time, morning/afternoon/evening greeting.
2. Head-to-lungs breathing visual.
3. Tap to begin a provisional 3-second inhale / 6-second exhale guide.
4. Pause/resume, skip, breathe at one's own pace, reduced movement.
5. Comfortable, unforced breathing instructions and a short stop-if-uncomfortable instruction.
6. A visible “Why this exercise?” disclosure with sources and limitations.

The lung animation is a pacing cue, not a measurement of breathing or an accurate simulation of lung volume. The prototype uses a simplified anatomical diagram pending illustration review.

## Not implemented

AI answers, clinical therapy, CBT exercises, user profiles, persistent journals, data collection, crisis detection, or public deployment. Skip acknowledges the choice within this screen; it does not enter an unfinished chatbot.

## Research requirement

Distinguish general breathing evidence, exact protocol evidence, age-specific evidence, and product-specific validation. Clinical review of the teen-facing breathing protocol is outstanding.
