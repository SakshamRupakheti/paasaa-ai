Why this exercise? Science & sources

Breathing

Guided breathing may help some people manage moments of stress. Teen findings are mixed. Paasaa and its exact rhythm have not been clinically tested.

  • Trial: 150 young people aged 12–13 — mixed physiological findings; no improvement in reported feelings or stress recovery compared with the control.
  • Pilot: 43 high-school students — anxiety changes were not statistically significant.
  • NHS breathing guidance — gentle, comfortable breathing without forcing it.

Self-monitoring

The check-in records feelings, body sensations, context, thoughts, and actions. These categories reflect parts of the NHS thought record. This version focuses on noticing, without challenging thoughts or making diagnoses. Its 0–10 questions are personal ratings, not validated diagnostic scales. The app has not been clinically tested, and adult guidance alone does not establish suitability for every teen.

Evidence checked September 29, 2026. Qualified clinical review, especially for younger users, remains outstanding.

You don’t have to handle this alone.

This page is not monitored and cannot assess risk or contact help for you. If you may act on thoughts of harm or are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency service now.

If you’re a young person, a trusted adult, school counsellor, or health professional can help you find support.

  • United States: call or text 988, available 24/7. 988 Lifeline
  • UK & Ireland: call 116 123 for Samaritans, day or night. Samaritans
  • UK, under 19: call Childline on 0800 1111. Childline
  • Other countries: find a local helpline.
