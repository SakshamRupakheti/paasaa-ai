3 seconds in · 6 seconds out

Keep your breath gentle. You don’t need to match the count perfectly.

If you feel dizzy, short of breath, or more uncomfortable, stop and breathe normally.

Why this exercise?

Guided breathing may help some people manage moments of stress. Results in teens are mixed, and this Paasaa exercise has not been clinically tested.

  • Trial: 150 young people aged 12–13 — some physiological effects, but no improvement in reported feelings or stress recovery compared with the control. It does not validate our exact 3-in/6-out rhythm.
  • Pilot: 43 high-school students — a five-week programme; anxiety changes were not statistically significant.
  • NHS breathing guidance — recommends comfortable, unforced breathing. This is practical guidance, not a trial of Paasaa.

Proposed rhythm: 3 seconds in, 6 seconds out. Suitability for younger users and the final instructions still need clinical review.
